On August 9, 1995, Aviateca Flight 901 crashed into the San Vicente volcano in El Salvador, due to extreme weather conditions and route deviations. All 65 people on board lost their lives.

This work pays tribute to the memory of the passengers, the crew, and the families affected by one of the worst air disasters in the region.

Flight 901 · Aviateca

A 360° recreation from a window seat, in tribute to the victims of August 9, 1995. The impact is not shown.

Use headphones: the sound is spatial. Drag to look around. Duration: about 90 seconds.

On a headset (Quest, Pico…), open this page in its browser and press “Begin in VR headset”. Inside the headset, camera shake and strobing are reduced to avoid motion sickness.
